The first 31 days

Brief to start date, with the days marked

These are the median timings from last year's placements. They are not a promise. If your brief needs a longer search, we will tell you before we start.

  1. Day 0

    Brief taken

    One hour on site or on a call. We ask what the last person in the role got wrong, and what the band actually buys in this market.

  2. Day 3

    Longlist

    Six to ten people in writing, with expectations and notice periods. If the band is wrong, this is where we say so, with the numbers behind it.

  3. Day 10

    Shortlist

    Four to six people, each one interviewed by us first. You get the notes and the reason every person is on the list.

  4. Day 14

    First interviews

    We book the rooms, brief both sides, and get feedback to everyone within 24 hours, including the people you pass on.

  5. Day 24

    Offer

    We run the counter-offer conversation. Roughly half our offers get one, so we start that work before the offer goes out.

  6. Day 31

    Start

    We call on day one and again on day thirty. If it is not working, the replacement is free inside 12 weeks.

Retained search runs longer, four to six weeks for a senior appointment. The stages are the same, the pace is not.

Straight answers

The questions we get asked on every call

If the answer you need is not here, call the desk. You will get a person who works that market, not a switchboard.

01What do you charge?

Contingency is 15% to 20% of first-year salary, invoiced on the start date. Retained search starts at 22% and is billed in three stages: on brief, on shortlist, on start. Temporary and contract placements are charged as an hourly rate with a margin of 32% to 38% built in.

Nothing is invoiced until someone starts work.

02What happens if the hire leaves?

Permanent placements carry a 12-week guarantee. If someone leaves, or is dismissed for cause, inside those 12 weeks, we replace them free of charge. Fixed-term placements carry 8 weeks.

The only conditions are that the brief has not changed and the invoice was paid within terms.

03How fast is a shortlist?

Three working days for a longlist in writing, ten for a shortlist of four to six people we have already interviewed. Those are medians from last year rather than a promise.

Retained search runs longer. Four to six weeks is normal for a senior appointment.

04Do you take every role you are offered?

No. We turned down 118 briefs last year, which is about a third of the ones we were sent. The usual reason is a band sitting 15% or more under the market.

When that happens we put it in writing with the salary data behind it, and the decision about whether to move the number stays with you.

05Do we have to give you exclusivity?

On the shortlist, yes. We will run a role alongside one other agency and that has never caused a problem. If a brief is going out to five or six agencies we will usually pass on it, because we work best when we are the one doing the search properly.

06What do you do with candidate data?

Your details are never sent to a client without your written permission, including for roles we think you would be perfect for. Records are held for 24 months, consent is re-confirmed every 12 months, and a deletion request is actioned within 30 days.

We are registered with the ICO and we are a corporate member of the REC.

07Is it free for candidates?

Yes. Employers pay us. You will never be asked for a fee, and you will never be put forward for anything without a conversation first.
