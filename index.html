Who we are

Why choose an HR firm that tells you when the numbers don't add up?

Great hiring starts with realistic expectations. Before we begin a search, we assess the role, salary range, market conditions, and available talent. If the budget does not match the profile you are looking for, we tell you upfront — so you can make informed decisions before investing time in the recruitment process.

  • Market expertise, one focus Our consultants specialise in specific markets, giving them a clear understanding of current hiring trends, talent availability, and salary expectations. Your brief is handled by someone who knows the market you are hiring in.
  • Clear salary benchmarking We benchmark your salary range against relevant market data before the search begins. If adjustments are needed, we explain why and provide practical insights to help you build a realistic and competitive hiring brief.

Our approach

Three steps, and the days marked

These are the median timings from last year's placements, not a promise. Retained search runs longer, and we say so before you commit.

  1. 01

    Brief and band check

    One hour with the person who can say yes, then the salary data in writing. Day 0 to day 3.

  2. 02

    Search and interview

    We interview everyone before you do. Longlist at day 3, shortlist of four to six at day 10.

  3. 03

    Offer and start

    We run the counter-offer conversation, then call on day one and day thirty. Day 31 start.
