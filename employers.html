How we work

Four things we do that other agencies talk about

We are not the cheapest way to hire. We are the way that stops you running the same brief for the fourth time, which is usually the more expensive option.

  • 01

    We tell you when the band is wrong

    In writing, with the data: what the last five hires in that job were paid, and what the people you want are earning now. You can ignore it. About a third of clients move the number, and those roles get filled.

  • 02

    One agency, or none

    We work a role alongside one other agency at most. If a brief is going to five or six, we will pass, because a six-way race produces the same three CVs faster and nobody does the search properly.

  • 03

    Everyone on the shortlist has been interviewed

    By us, on the phone or in person, before the CV reaches you. You get the notes and the reason each person is on the list, including the reservations.

  • 04

    We take about a third of the briefs we are sent

    118 briefs went back last year. Sometimes it is the band, sometimes it is a role that does not exist in the market, and sometimes it is a client we cannot work with. You will know which within a day.

Timings

What we owe you, and what we need back

Our side

  1. Day 0

    Brief taken

    One hour, on site or on a call, with the person who can actually say yes.

  2. Day 1

    Written band check

    What the last five hires in this job were paid, and what the people you want are earning now.

  3. Day 3

    Longlist

    Six to ten people in writing, with salary expectations and notice periods.

  4. Day 10

    Shortlist

    Four to six, each one interviewed by us first. You get the notes and the reservations.

  5. Day 14

    First interviews

    We book the rooms, brief both sides, and get feedback to everyone inside 24 hours.

  6. Day 24

    Offer

    We run the counter-offer conversation, which about half of our offers attract.

  7. Day 31

    Start, then day one and day thirty

    Two check-in calls, and the 12-week guarantee standing behind the placement.

Your side

  • A band agreed in writing before we approach anyone, even if it is a range.
  • One person who can say yes, named on the brief. Not a panel, not a committee.
  • Feedback inside 48 hours after every interview, even if it is one line.
  • Two interview slots held in the first three weeks, so the good candidates do not drift.
  • Honesty about the last person who did the job. It changes who we look for.

When a role stalls, it is almost always one of those five missing rather than a shortage of people. We will say so on the call rather than in a quarterly review.

The guarantee

If it does not work, we do it again for nothing

Every permanent placement carries a 12-week guarantee. If the person you appoint leaves, or is dismissed for cause, inside those twelve weeks, we run the search again and you pay nothing for the replacement. Fixed-term placements carry eight weeks.

There are two conditions, and both are on the invoice: the brief has not changed since the shortlist was issued, and the invoice was paid within its terms. If the role changed after you met the shortlist, that is a new search rather than a failed one, and we will say so before we start.

Called on 11 placements last year, out of 144. Nine of the eleven were refilled inside three weeks.
